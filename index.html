Who Is Jesus?

Jesus is the central figure of Christianity. Christians believe that Jesus taught people to love God and love one another, and that His life, death, and resurrection are at the heart of the Christian faith.

Gospel Glow is a school project created to share videos and information about Jesus in a welcoming and easy-to-understand way.
